# Key Findings

_Discoveries, surprises, and notable results. Updated as analysis progresses._

### 2026-03-26: Migration declined in every major Western destination country simultaneously
Net migration fell from post-pandemic peak to latest: Canada -114%, NZ -91%, Germany -84%, UK -78%, Netherlands -58%, Australia -43%, Ireland -25%. The pattern is universal.

### 2026-03-26: The US immigration decline is modest by global standards
US net migration per capita peaked at 8.3/1000 — vs Canada at 31.5/1000. The US surge was large in absolute terms but small relative to population.

### 2026-03-27: India is the #1 or #2 source country for 4 of 5 major destinations
Canada, UK, Australia, US all draw heavily from India. When all four crack down simultaneously, India bears the brunt — unprecedented.

### 2026-03-27: The crackdown is bipartisan across ideologies
Center-left Canada (Liberals), Labour UK, Labor Australia, SPD-led Germany, center-right Netherlands — every government regardless of ideology converged on the same restrictionist policy. Not a left-right issue.

### 2026-03-28: Mexico is flat as a US immigration source; India is the growth story
Mexico's US foreign-born population: -28K over 6 years (2018-2024). India: +510K. The pipeline that actually matters for US long-term workforce growth is Indian, not Mexican.

### 2026-03-28: Canada's population actually declined in 2025
First time in modern records. Swung from +744K (2024) to -102K (2025), driven by temporary resident departures.

### 2026-03-29: Nobody has written the definitive "global crackdown" piece
OECD documented data. Individual outlets covered individual countries. But no major publication has connected the dots: this is a synchronized, cross-ideological, global policy convergence. Gap in the literature.

### 2026-03-29: Portugal is the European Canada
Immigration surged 155% (2018-2023), then government abolished easy-entry mechanisms, raised naturalization from 5 to 10 years, planned 23,000 removals. Most dramatic European boom-bust.
