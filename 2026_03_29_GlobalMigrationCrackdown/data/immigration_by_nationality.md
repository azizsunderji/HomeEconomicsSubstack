## METHODOLOGICAL NOTES

1. **Comparability across countries**: Each destination uses different definitions:
   - Canada: NOW COMPREHENSIVE (PR + study + work permits by nationality) -- but double-counts overlap
   - US: LPR (green cards) + CBP encounters -- two separate systems, different concepts
   - UK: ONS long-term immigration (intending to stay 12+ months) -- broadest/best definition, by nationality
   - Australia: NOW COMPREHENSIVE (ABS Net Overseas Migration by country of birth) -- gold standard
   - Germany: registered arrivals (Zuzuge) -- comprehensive, but nationality breakdown limited to 2024

2. **Best measures for total immigration by nationality**:
   - UK: ONS LTIM by nationality (Tables 3e/3f) -- Year Ending June, covers all visa types
   - Australia: ABS NOM by country of birth -- Calendar Year, covers all visa types
   - Canada: IRCC study + work + PR by citizenship -- but has double-counting
   - Germany: AZR stock changes as proxy (imperfect) or 2024 arrivals by nationality
   - US: No single comprehensive source; must combine LPRs + CBP + refugee/asylee data
   - NZ: Stats NZ by citizenship -- only two comparison years available (2019, 2023)

3. **COVID-19 impact**: All countries show 2020-2021 depression. Recovery timing varies.

4. **2024-2025 slowdown visible everywhere**:
   - UK: Non-EU+ immigration down 43% from YE Jun 23 peak to YE Jun 25
   - Australia: NOM down 38% from CY2023 peak to CY2024
   - NZ: Net migration down 81% from 2023 to 2024
   - Canada: Study permits down 24%, work permits down 5% (2023 to 2024)
   - Germany: Net migration down 35% (2023 to 2024)
   - Netherlands: Total immigration down 6% (2023 to 2024)

5. **India is the dominant story**:
   - #1 source for Canada (585K total in 2024), UK (143K immigration YE Jun 25), Australia (+59K NOM in 2024), NZ
   - #2 for US (after Mexico), growing in Germany
   - But India flows are declining everywhere: down from peaks in all destinations
   - Indian emigration from UK rising sharply (74K YE Jun 25, up from 23K YE Jun 21)
